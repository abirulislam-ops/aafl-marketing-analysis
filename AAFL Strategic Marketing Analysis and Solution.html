Summary

Q1 key findings (each one has a Q2 solution in section 6)

  1. Growth is real. Jan–Jul'26 volume is up 90% on 2025 while the industry grew 5.1%, the fastest of all named companies.
  2. Q1 beat plan. 104% of the volume target: July missed (96%), August and September beat it (108–109%).
  3. Marketing-led revenue beat the 30% target. August (2.22×) and September (1.95×) exceeded it; July (0.55×) fell short.
  4. Profit ROMI ranged 17.1×–58.6× by campaign period; the overall topline ROMI is 19.9×.
  5. Spend shifted away from brand building. ATL used 5% and Digital 28% of plan, while BTL ran at 430% and Gift & Printing at 261%.
  6. To reach #13 (Provita), Akij needs about 5,930 MT more per month than its Q1 run-rate.
  7. Draft focus: lead with cattle feed, then fish feed; hold position in poultry.
