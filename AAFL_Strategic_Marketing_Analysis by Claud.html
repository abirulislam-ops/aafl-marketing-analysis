Key findings

  1. Growth is real. H1'26 volume is up 88% on 2025 while the industry grew 4.8%, the fastest of all named companies.
  2. Q1 beat plan. 104% of the volume target: July missed (96%), August and September beat it (108–109%).
  3. Marketing paid back about 4x in gross profit against the Jan–Jun'26 sales run-rate. July did not pay back (−0.2x).
  4. The current ROMI workbook overstates returns and cannot show which campaign worked.
  5. Spend shifted away from brand building. ATL used 5% and Digital 28% of plan, while BTL ran at 430% and Gift & Printing at 261%.
  6. To reach #13 (Provita), Akij needs about 6,150 MT more per month than its Q1 run-rate.
  7. Draft focus: lead with cattle feed, then fish feed; hold position in poultry.

Why the current workbook overstates ROMI

  • Base too low: 28.48 Cr/month vs an actual run-rate of ~89 Cr, so sales that existed before Q1 are counted as marketing-led.
  • Revenue split by spend share: every campaign in a month gets the same ROMI, so the sheet measures the month, not the campaign.
  • Double counting: Profile Light Box claims 100% of Aug–Sep incremental revenue that Aug and Sep campaigns also claim. Campaign rows add up to ৳402.6 Cr against ৳55.2 Cr of real incremental revenue.
  • Total row cut short: spend sums only rows 3–12 (৳80.3 lakh of ৳174.0 lakh), which is why profit ROMI (24.9x) shows higher than revenue ROMI (19.9x).
  • Spend mismatches with the Budget Tracker (T-shirt, dealer benefit, profile light box, Model Farm lines swapped).

What the competitor data says

  • Provita is the closest model: it almost doubled (+97%) to 3.8% share in six months, the same jump Akij is attempting.
  • Kazi is losing volume (−31%) and Aftab (−9%), so share is available in the 3–4% tier.
  • New Hope and ACI grew ~19–20%, the strongest growth among the top ten.
  • Akij's Q1 run-rate (18,253 MT) already beats its FY target average (17,500) and would put it at ~2.9% share.
